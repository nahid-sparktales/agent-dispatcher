Recommend SQLite. The app needs atomic multi-record updates and unique SKU lookups within an offline single-writer process (docs/requirements.md). SQLite offers transactions, UNIQUE constraints and indexes through the standard-library sqlite3 module (docs/sqlite.md). JSON offers readable whole-file data but would require application-built uniqueness checks and a careful atomic-replacement protocol; naive writes have no supplied crash-safety guarantee (docs/json.md). SQLite adds schema migration and database-error handling work (docs/sqlite.md). Neither source contains measured latency, so I make no speed claim. Verify rollback on a failed batch, duplicate SKU rejection, lookup correctness, restart recovery and a representative 20,000-record workload before claiming performance.
